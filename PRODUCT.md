# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Associates and cashiers** on the shop floor: standing, often holding an iPad or working a counter till, a client watching the screen. Interrupted constantly. Need a sale done in a few taps, with the client's history at hand, without ever losing a sale to a dropped network.
- **Store managers**: approve overrides on someone else's device, open and close drawers, watch today's numbers between clients, move stock between stores.
- **Owners and head office**: a laptop or desktop in the evening. Compare stores, staff and categories, set roles, brand every store, connect an AI assistant (MCP) to ask questions and make audited changes.
- Verticals: luxury boutiques first (serialized pieces, clienteling, high tickets), then any retail or hospitality counter that wants its own look.

## Product Purpose

A universal point of sale that every store can make its own: its colours, type, words and register layout, on iOS, Android, web, Windows, macOS and Linux from one codebase. Success means a cashier finishes a sale faster than on Square or Shopify POS, a sale is never lost offline, a manager trusts the numbers because they reconcile to the same ledger, and an owner can re-brand or re-arrange a store without a developer.

## Brand Personality

Composed, exact, discreet. The software stays in the background so the store's brand and the client relationship are in front. It speaks plainly, in the store's own vocabulary ("Client", "Maison", "Purchase"), and never makes a cashier feel slow or blamed.

## Anti-references

- Legacy POS grids: rainbow button walls, beveled keys, tiny type, modal on top of modal.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: hero-metric tiles with gradient accents, identical card grids, decorative charts that answer nothing.
- Cream/sand "luxury" pastiche: parchment backgrounds, gold everywhere, serif labels on buttons.
- Consumer-app playfulness: bouncy motion, confetti, emoji status.

## Design Principles

1. **The sale is sacred.** Item count, total and the pay action are always visible and always in the same place; nothing on the register can hide them.
2. **The brand owns the surface, the system owns legibility.** Stores choose colours, fonts and layout; the token resolver guarantees contrast, target sizes and state colours no matter what they choose.
3. **One ledger, one truth.** Every number on a dashboard drills down to the orders and movements that produce it.
4. **Offline is a normal state, not an error.** Show what is queued, never block the floor on the network.
5. **Keyboard, touch and scanner are equal citizens.** Every primary action has a shortcut on desktop and a thumb-reachable target on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 controls, 44 to 56 px targets by density, visible focus, reduced motion honoured on every platform, screen-reader labels on all icon actions and chart summaries as text. Status is never colour-only (icon or word always accompanies it). Tabular numerals for every amount.
